Forge Ledger

A private portfolio tracker for personal use.

What it is

Forge Ledger is an Android app built and used by one person to see their own bank balances and investment holdings in a single view. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads account balances and investment holdings from the owner's own financial accounts through Plaid. It does not move money and has no ability to make trades or payments.

How the data is handled

  • Data is stored only on the owner's phone, protected by the phone's encryption and an app lock.
  • Access credentials are stored encrypted in the Android Keystore.
  • Data is never sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • Linked accounts can be disconnected at any time from within the app or through Plaid Portal, and all stored data can be erased from the app's settings.

Privacy

Because the app is used only by its owner, there is no collection of anyone else's personal information. This page is published to describe the app's purpose and data practices to financial institutions.
